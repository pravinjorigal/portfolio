Business context

Registration was a member’s first interaction with a digital life-insurance platform. The journey had to validate customer and policy data, coordinate with identity providers, and create secure access—while presenting a responsive, simple experience to the user.

The challenge

Multiple enterprise systems had to participate before a registration could complete. Those systems could not be changed; customer data required careful handling; and the release plan was fixed. The service also had to evolve without breaking agreed client contracts.

My contribution

I owned the backend implementation: translating requirements into REST APIs and business logic, integrating identity systems, publishing mock APIs for frontend progress, supporting QA and UAT, coordinating production activity, and handling post-release validation.

Solution

We defined stable API contracts early. That let frontend work proceed against mock implementations while dependent systems were still becoming available. Once integrations were ready, I replaced mocks with production services without changing the frontend contract.

The resulting workflow validated business information, retrieved policy-related context, and coordinated identity provisioning through the enterprise environment. Redis was used where frequently accessed reference data could be served consistently with fewer backend lookups.

Engineering decisions

  • API-first: protected parallel team delivery and reduced integration rework.
  • Mock APIs: removed dependency wait time during frontend development and testing.
  • REST: suited an interactive flow where users need immediate feedback.
  • Centralized identity: aligned account lifecycle and authentication with the wider enterprise.

Operational learning

After release, structured application logs and Splunk were essential for following requests across the dependent systems. Clear correlation and defensive integration handling made registration problems easier to diagnose without exposing sensitive data.

What I would improve today

I would add end-to-end request correlation and distributed tracing, resilience tests for dependent services, Resilience4j circuit breakers and bulkheads, plus dedicated observability dashboards for the registration funnel.

Recruiter summary

Delivered production-ready REST APIs for enterprise customer onboarding, integrating identity and business systems while enabling frontend and backend teams to work in parallel through stable API contracts.
